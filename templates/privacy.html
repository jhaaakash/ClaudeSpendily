Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 17, 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create an account, we collect your name and email address. As you use Spendly, we store the expense records you enter — including amounts, categories, dates, and any descriptions you provide. We do not collect payment information or any data beyond what is necessary to run the service.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to provide and improve Spendly. This includes displaying your expense history, generating summaries, and maintaining your account. We do not use your financial data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to the service you signed up for.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely on our servers. We apply industry-standard measures to protect it from unauthorised access, loss, or disclosure. Your account password is stored as a one-way hash and is never readable by anyone, including us. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time.

4. Third-Party Services

Spendly does not sell or share your personal data with third parties. We may use infrastructure providers (such as hosting or database services) to operate the platform; these providers process data only on our behalf and under confidentiality obligations. No financial data is shared with advertisers or analytics platforms.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us. We are committed to addressing any concerns promptly and transparently.
